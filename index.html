About

The person holding the room, so you can be in it.

Most ceremonies sound like templates. Yours shouldn't.

I meet you where your story actually is — how you met, the inside jokes, the thing your families still don't know — and turn it into a ceremony people remember. No recycled readings, no sermon, no surprises you haven't approved in writing.

And when the ceremony ends, I don't disappear. I run the reception as your MC: entrances, toasts, timeline, the "please find your seats" moments — so your planner can plan and your family can relax.

FAQ

Things couples always ask.

Something else on your mind? Ask me directly — I answer my own email.

Are you actually ordained? Can we legally marry?

Yes — ordained and in good standing, with ceremonies performed and licenses filed across multiple states. If you're planning a symbolic ceremony abroad, we'll sort the legal part at home first and I'll walk you through it.

Do you really travel anywhere?

That's the whole idea. I work nationwide and for destination weddings, with travel quoted at cost and agreed upfront — never a surprise line item after you've committed.

Can you do bilingual ceremonies?

Yes — English, 中文, or a natural blend of both, switching seamlessly so neither side of the family misses a beat. It's one of the most requested things I do.

How long is a typical ceremony?

Twenty to thirty minutes. Long enough to feel like it mattered, short enough that nobody quietly checks their phone.

How far in advance should we book?

Six to twelve months for peak season (May through October) is comfortable. Elopements often come together in a month or two — if your date is close, ask anyway. I keep room in the calendar.

What about deposits and cancellations?

A 30% deposit holds your date, with the balance due two weeks before the wedding. If you cancel more than 90 days out, the deposit comes back to you in full.
